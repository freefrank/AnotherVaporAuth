TITLE:
AVA: Steam Guard Authenticator

SHORT:
Unofficial open-source Steam Guard authenticator. Codes, trades, market.

FULL:
AVA is an unofficial, open-source Steam Guard authenticator. It is a community
project and is not affiliated with Valve or Steam in any way.

Your authenticator lives on your device, encrypted. AVA is built for people who
want their Steam two-factor codes and trade confirmations in one place they
control.

WHAT IT DOES

• Steam Guard codes — a live list with countdown rings and tap-to-copy.
• Trade confirmations — approve or decline trades and market listings, in
  batches, without an embedded browser.
• Trade offers — received, sent and history, with both sides' items shown, and
  gift / one-sided / escrow warnings before you accept.
• Sign-in approval — approve Steam logins from inside AVA, with the device and
  location shown, just like the official app.
• Inventory and Community Market — browse your inventory and list items with
  live Steam fees and price history.
• Redeem Steam keys — activate a product key on any account you hold.
• Device management — see every device signed into your account and sign any of
  them out remotely.
• Steam Family groups — invites with pre-join checks, and a group overview.

MIGRATION

AVA reads and writes the classic .maFile format (PBKDF2/SHA1 + AES-256-CBC), so
authenticators from Steam Desktop Authenticator and similar tools move over
unchanged. Exports from Steam++ / Watt Toolkit are imported too. You can export
your .maFile at any time — your data is never locked in.

SECURITY

• A 6-digit PIN guards a random 256-bit key held in your device's hardware
  keystore; local storage is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
• Fingerprint or device-PIN unlock.
• Every irreversible action — accepting a trade, joining a family group — takes
  a deliberate press-and-hold, not a stray tap.
• Codes work offline. Fonts and assets are bundled; nothing is downloaded at
  runtime.

AVA Pro (optional) unlocks the Neon and Pixel skins and removes ads. The free
version shows ads on Google Play. Everything to do with your Steam account works
the same either way.
